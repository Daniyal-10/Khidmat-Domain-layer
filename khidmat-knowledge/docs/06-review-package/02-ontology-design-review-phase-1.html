4 Completed Work

Two stages, presented with their decisions, assumptions and unresolved questions.

4.1 Phase 1 — Domain Primitives

Summary

A Domain Primitive is a category of concept — the kind of thing a humanitarian concept can be. Not a humanitarian concept itself. Eight were derived by asking, of every concept in the Reference Model, what kind of thing is this? until answers stopped multiplying.

PrimitiveDefinitionEvidence
IdentityThat which persists and must be re-identified across encountersModerate
RelationA connection between things that persistModerate (institutional) · none (social)
ConditionThat which is true across a span of time and can changeLimited
OccurrenceThat which happened at a point in timeModerate
ContextThe frame relative to which a statement holdsStrong
NormThat which bounds what is permitted, required or validStrong
EvidenceThat which grounds a beliefLimited
Epistemic StanceThe warrant held for what is asserted — including what is unknownModerate

Key decisions

Categories, not concrete things

Person is not a primitive. Primitives sit above the Entities layer, so Person would be both source and member of it.

Set closed once ratified

A concept needing a new primitive is escalated, not accommodated. The set is not yet ratified.

One primary classification

A concept needing two primitives is treated as information, not resolved by dual placement.

Domain scope: all of it

Every part of described humanitarian reality is in domain scope, with actions admitted under constraint.

Coverage test

The set was tested against the giving side — the part of the business flow with no domain description. Donor classifies as Identity, a gift as Occurrence, adoption as Relation, a funding restriction as Norm. No new primitive is required. This converts the giving gap from structural to content-only: the categories survive its arrival even though its content is undescribed.

Why this test matters

Under the alternative definition — primitives as concrete things — Donor, Gift, Adoption and Restriction would each have been candidate primitives and the set would have broken. This is the strongest independent support for the definition chosen.

Assumptions

Disclosure

Phase 1 does not yet carry a formal assumptions register, although Phase 2 does. At least four judgements qualify: the category reading, closure at eight members, the eight candidate rejections, and treating the giving-side test as decisive. This inconsistency is known and outstanding.

Open tensions

ConceptTensionWhy unresolved
RiskCondition or Epistemic StanceCannot be settled by evidence — the Reference Model's risk section has no external corroboration at all. A ruling, not a research question.
NeedCondition or RelationDefined relationally but behaves like a condition. Entangled with the undefined "basic standard of wellbeing".

Provenance disclosure

Stated openly

The eight categories match a set produced before the foundation reset. They were re-derived from the Reference Model, but that earlier file had been read during the same working session, so independence cannot be certified. The mitigation is that the derivation trace is fully auditable — the set can be judged on its merits regardless of origin. Independent re-derivation is recommended before ratification.

4.2 Phase 2 — Ontology Layers

Layer derivation

Every layer names the primitive it derives from. All eight primitives are consumed; no layer introduces a category absent from Phase 1.

LayerDerives fromHoldsEvidence
1 · FacetsCondition + ContextThe dimensions along which things varySplit
2 · EntitiesIdentityThings that persist and are re-identifiedModerate
3 · RelationshipsRelationConnections between entitiesSplit
4 · ConstraintsNorm, scoped by ContextWhat is permitted, required or validStrong
5 · StatesConditionValues a condition takes over timeLimited
6 · EventsOccurrenceWhat happened at a pointModerate
7 · CognitionEpistemic Stance + EvidenceThe warrant for what other layers assertModerate
8 · CoordinationRelation + Occurrence + Context + NormRecurring multi-party shapesStrong where evidenced

Key decisions

Facets and States are separate

A facet is the axis; a state is the value on it. The domain has dimensions almost everywhere and values almost nowhere — the split makes that visible.

Actions are quarantined

Registration, verification and delivery appear in the model but may never be its organising axis.

Cognition carries the unknown

Absence of information is modelled as knowledge, distinct from a negative finding.

Values are not invented

Where the domain names a dimension and not its values, no values were supplied.

Worked example — the one externally corroborated structure

Support classifies on three independent axes — Sector × Modality × Temporal Phase — whose intersection identifies an intervention. The prior single flattened list mixed two of these axes together. This is the pattern a correct facet structure follows.

The Cognition seam

Because Risk's classification is unresolved, Cognition is built around the uncertainty rather than over it:

If Risk is ruled…Risk lives inCognition holds
ConditionStatesOnly the confidence in a risk assessment
Epistemic StanceCognitionRisk itself — horizon, trend and severity

Cognition defines expectation about a future state as a first-class form, so the ruling is a single documented switch rather than a rewrite.

One structural caution, stated before it is asked

Eight primitives produced exactly eight layers consuming all eight primitives. That fit is not evidence of correctness — the layer set was given, and the primitive categories were derived to stand above it, so the completeness of the mapping is expected rather than confirmatory. The genuine independent test of the primitive set is the giving-side coverage test in §4.1, which it passed and which the alternative definition would have failed.

5 Methodology Review Requested

Seven decisions requiring confirmation. These are review items, not implementation issues.

R-1 · Highest priority

Confirm the definition of a Domain Primitive

The Domain Reference Model states a ruling was required before primitive discovery. It was not obtained, and the work proceeded under a ruling the authors made: a primitive is a category of concept (Identity, Relation, Condition), not a concrete irreducible (Person, Household, Need).

Reasoning: stage 2 builds a layer named Entities, and layers derive from primitives. If Person were a primitive it would be both the source and a member of that layer, and the derivation would be circular.

If ruled otherwise: both stages are void and must be rebuilt from the definition upward. Nothing downstream survives.
Resolved 2026-09-02 by Stage 7 Governance ruling G3 — Opt 1 (category of concept) ratified. See `07-STAGE-7-GOVERNANCE-DECISIONS.md`.
R-2

Ratify the domain scope decision

The scope decision was performed retrospectively and concluded that all of described humanitarian reality is in domain scope — with actions admitted under constraint, and support at dimension level only. This keeps the giving side inside the model, consistent with the earlier decision to model reality fully and sequence implementation separately.

If narrowed: affected layers contract. The primitive set is unaffected — the coverage test established it survives either way.
R-3 · Blocking

Rule on the classification of Risk

Risk is either a Condition (being at risk is currently true of a household) or an Epistemic Stance (a warranted expectation about a future condition). The domain reference asserts both readings in different places: risk "produces signals and does not decide" is stance language; risk being "first-class, not a byproduct of severity rules" resists demotion to an annotation.

This cannot be resolved by further evidence review — the risk material carries no external corroboration whatsoever.

Blocking: stage 3 cannot proceed. A pillar concerning risk or safety cannot be derived while the layer holding Risk is undetermined.
R-4

Rule on the classification of Need

A need is defined as a gap between current state and a basic standard of wellbeing — relational language — but behaves as a condition that opens, changes severity and resolves. It is currently held under the condition reading, provisionally.

Note: the current placement conveniently avoids requiring the wellbeing standard to be defined. That is a reason for caution, not comfort.
R-5

Confirm the treatment of concepts spanning two layers

Phase 1 forbids dual classification and requires escalation instead. Phase 2 nonetheless places several concepts in more than one layer. Either the rule should permit a concept to have one primary classification plus named derived aspects, or these cases should be escalated as further tensions.

Recommendation: permit aspectual presence under a stated composition rule. Amend Phase 1 rather than override it downstream.
R-6

Confirm the divergence on Organisation and Programme

The Business Logic Blueprint treats these as a single actor. Every external source examined names implementing organisations separately from the programmes they lead. The layers follow the evidence and separate them.

If the Blueprint governs: the two collapse into one entity and the constraining relationship between them disappears.
R-7

Acknowledge the evidence asymmetry and its consequence

The six evidence dossiers examine how humanitarian organisations operate — actors, tensions, lifecycle, capabilities, services, interventions. None examines people, families, households, situations, needs or risk. Consequently the primitives classifying organisational reality are well evidenced, and those classifying human reality are not.

Two primitives — Condition and Relation — carry most of the human content and rest on a single unvalidated source. No statement anywhere in this project has been validated by a humanitarian practitioner.

Consequence: the primitive set can be worked with but must not be closed or ratified on this evidence. Closure asserts completeness the evidence does not support.

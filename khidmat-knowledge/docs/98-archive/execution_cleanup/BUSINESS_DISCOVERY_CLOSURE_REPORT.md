# Business Discovery Closure Report

## Executive Summary
This document formally records the closure of the Business Discovery phase for the Khidmat Knowledge Repository. All scheduled Topic Dossiers (TD-01 through TD-06) have been completed, evidence has been gathered and synthesized, and the Human Owner has rendered binding decisions on all open contradictions. The repository is now certified as ready to transition into the Khidmat Foundation Synthesis phase. 

## Discovery Scope Completed
The following Topic Dossiers were executed and handed off:
- **TD-01 — Humanitarian Ecosystem & Actor Types**: Categorized core humanitarian actors by mandate and role.
- **TD-02 — Stakeholder Interests & Tensions**: Highlighted the structural tension between donor accountability and beneficiary dignity.
- **TD-03 — Humanitarian Lifecycle (Business View)**: Differentiated the beneficiary case lifecycle from the organizational programmatic cycle.
- **TD-04 — Business Capabilities**: Confirmed MEAL and CFM as standard capabilities and exposed the gap between organizational and case functions.
- **TD-05 — Business Services & Value Streams**: Established that value streams split between material and information flows across different altitudes.
- **TD-06 — Intervention Categories**: Categorized interventions across Sector, Modality, and Temporal phase.

## Evidence Summary
Evidence gathering successfully utilized Tier B (Sector standards and bodies), Tier C (Project-internal artifacts), and Tier D (Secondary literature). Tier A (Direct practitioner elicitation) remained structurally unavailable during this phase. Despite the lack of primary field data, external Tier B and D sources robustly corroborated the repository's internal principles, particularly regarding accountability loops and the structural difference between case and programmatic lifecycles.

## Assumptions Summary
Due to the absence of Tier A evidence, 10 active assumptions (AR-001 through AR-010) remain logged in the Assumption Register. These assumptions are necessary to bridge the gap between abstract standards and operational reality. They carry structural weight into the Business Master Plan and must be treated as explicit hypotheses subject to future operational validation.

## Contradiction Resolution Summary
All known structural contradictions have been successfully resolved by the Human Owner:
- **CL-001 (Programme vs. Organisation)**: RESOLVED. Evaluated as distinct business concepts (the implementing body vs. the bounded initiative), aligning with external sector standards.
- **CL-002 (Donor Status)**: RESOLVED. Confirmed as a valid humanitarian business actor. The exclusion of donor-facing functionality in V1 is confirmed as strictly a software implementation-scope decision, not a denial of humanitarian reality.

## Governance Status
The Governance Baseline v1.0 has been successfully established and codified in `CONSTITUTION.md` and `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md`. The Governance layer is complete, internally consistent, authoritative, and capable of governing the subsequent Khidmat Foundation and Ontology Design phases.

## Key Discoveries
The most critical insights forming the Khidmat Foundation are:
1. **Dual-Track Lifecycles**: Humanitarian business reality operates on two distinct, non-conflatable altitudes: the beneficiary case lifecycle (non-linear, human-centric) and the programmatic response cycle (linear, organization-centric).
2. **Accountability Tensions**: A persistent, structural tension exists between upwards accountability to donors (compliance, standardization) and downwards accountability to beneficiaries (dignity, localization).
3. **Actor Precision**: Implementing organizations and the funding initiatives (programmes) they run are distinct operational structures that must be modeled independently.

## Remaining Risks
1. **Absence of Tier A Validation**: The reliance on assumptions in place of direct practitioner evidence poses a risk to the operational accuracy of the downstream Business Architecture and Intervention Catalogues.
2. **Intervention Catalogue Detail**: While TD-06 defined intervention categories, the concrete, detailed intervention catalogue remains a dependency requiring operational input from programme staff.

## Lessons Learned
1. **Methodological Discipline**: The strict enforcement of phase gates (blocking architecture until discovery was complete and contradictions were resolved) successfully prevented premature ontological engineering and forced the resolution of critical business questions.
2. **Evidence Structuring**: Future repository work should continue to explicitly decouple the recording of evidence (dossiers) from the synthesis of rules (master plans), as this separation of concerns maintained clarity during contradiction resolution.

## Formal Certification
The Business Discovery phase has achieved its objectives. Sufficient evidence has been gathered to support the authoring of the Business Master Plan. 

**Business Discovery is hereby declared CLOSED.**

**The Khidmat Knowledge Repository is certified ready to begin Khidmat Foundation Synthesis.**

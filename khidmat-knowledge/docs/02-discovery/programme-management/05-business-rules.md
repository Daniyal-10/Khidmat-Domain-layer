# Business Rules

## Universal Policies
- Principles of impartiality, neutrality, and independence in programme design.

## Donor Policies
- Strict rules governing how specific funds can be used (e.g., restricted vs. unrestricted funding, origin constraints).

## Regional / Government Policies
- National disaster response frameworks dictating permitted intervention types.

## Organisation Policies
- Internal strategic mandates (e.g., "We only do WASH and Health").

## Programme Policies
- Specific constraints formulated for a single initiative.

## Constraints
- **Financial:** Strict budget ceilings and rigid grant cycles.
- **Regulatory:** Host government restrictions.
- **Operational:** Market capacity limitations.
- **Donor:** Earmarked funds.

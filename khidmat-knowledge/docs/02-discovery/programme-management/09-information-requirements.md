# Information Requirements

- **Population Assessments:** Macro-level data on community vulnerabilities.
- **Funding Availability:** Financial constraints and grant conditions.
- **Strategic Objectives:** Organisational or national response goals.
- **Operational Constraints:** Security, market, or logistical limitations on the ground.
- **Programme Performance:** Burn rates and macro-level output reports.
- **Humanitarian Indicators:** Standardized metrics justifying intervention.

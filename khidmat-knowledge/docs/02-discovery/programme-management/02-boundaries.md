# Domain Boundaries: Programme Management

## Owns
- Programme definition.
- Intervention catalogue.
- Eligibility policy.
- Prioritisation.
- Resource and budget planning.
- Programme adaptation and monitoring.

## Consumes
- **From Accountability:** Systemic learning reports, evaluations, community feedback.
- **From Case Management:** Aggregated rejection rates or rapid uptake.
- **From Resource & Logistics:** Market feasibility, procurement delays.
- **From Cross-Organisational Coordination:** Gap analyses avoiding overlap.

## Produces
- **For Case Management:** Intervention Catalogue, Eligibility Rules, operational budgets.
- **For Resource & Logistics:** Forecasted demand for material goods.
- **For Accountability:** Strategic objectives and outcome indicators.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Individual case assessment.
- Aid delivery.
- Procurement, warehouse operations, logistics.
- Impact evaluation.
- Cross-organisation coordination.

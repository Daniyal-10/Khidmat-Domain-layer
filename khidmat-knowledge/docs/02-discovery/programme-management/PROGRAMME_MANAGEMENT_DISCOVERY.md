# Programme Management Domain Discovery (Executive Narrative)

The Programme Management Domain exists to govern the macro-level coordination, resourcing, and strategic boundaries of humanitarian assistance. It solves the problem of allocating finite resources against systemic vulnerabilities by defining what assistance will be provided, to whom it is available, and under what conditions. It bridges the gap between institutional funding mandates and frontline case management operations.

Case Management cannot perform these responsibilities because it operates at the altitude of the individual; a case worker cannot assess global community needs or independently decide how to spend an organisation's entire budget without macro-level rules and constraints.

The successful outcomes of this domain include strategic alignment, resource efficacy, governed execution via actionable eligibility rules and intervention catalogues, and adaptive strategy to shifting ground realities.

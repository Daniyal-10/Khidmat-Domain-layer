# Business Events

- **Programme Created:** A new programmatic initiative is drafted.
- **Funding Secured:** A grant or budget is formally committed.
- **Programme Approved:** The programme is authorized to commence operations.
- **Intervention Added / Removed:** The catalogue is updated.
- **Eligibility Updated:** Rules governing access are modified.
- **Budget Allocated:** Funds are distributed to specific intervention lines.
- **Programme Expanded:** Target populations or geographic areas are increased.
- **Programme Suspended:** Operations are halted due to security or compliance issues.
- **Programme Revised:** Major strategic shifts in objectives or modalities.
- **Programme Closed:** The initiative reaches its end date or exhausts funding.

# Business Lifecycles

The Programme Cycle represents a macro-level lifecycle:
1. **Needs Prioritisation & Planning:** Designing objectives, targets, and budgets.
2. **Approval & Securing Funding:** Grant planning and final authorization.
3. **Implementation Oversight:** Strategic monitoring and budget tracking.
4. **Programme Revision/Adaptation:** Adjusting allocations and rules mid-cycle based on reality.
5. **Programme Closure:** Formally ending operations.

*(This lifecycle governs the Programme itself, distinct from the individual case lifecycles in Case Management).*

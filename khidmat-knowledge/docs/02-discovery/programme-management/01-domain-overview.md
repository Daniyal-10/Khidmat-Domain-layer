# Domain Overview: Programme Management

## Purpose
Governs the macro-level coordination, resourcing, and strategic boundaries of humanitarian assistance.

## Mission
To allocate finite resources against systemic vulnerabilities by defining what assistance will be provided, to whom it is available, and under what conditions.

## Scope
Bridges the gap between institutional funding mandates and frontline operations.

## Responsibilities
- Programme Design & Strategy Formulation
- Intervention Catalogue Management
- Eligibility Policy Definition
- Resource Prioritisation & Allocation
- Strategic Monitoring & Adaptation

## Business Outcomes
- Strategic alignment of funding to verified population needs.
- Optimal resource efficacy across competing crises.
- Governed execution via actionable eligibility rules and intervention catalogues.

## Business Capabilities
- Programme Design & Strategy Formulation
- Intervention Catalogue Management
- Eligibility Policy Definition
- Resource Prioritisation & Allocation
- Strategic Monitoring & Adaptation

## Core Business Activities
- Analyzing macro-level population vulnerability assessments.
- Drafting and approving overarching programme mandates.
- Allocating finite budgets across diverse intervention categories.
- Defining the precise technical rules for who qualifies for aid.
- Continuously adjusting rules and budgets based on burn rates and shifting ground realities.

# Business Language

## Preferred Terms
- **Programme:** The overarching strategic initiative.
- **Intervention Catalogue:** The menu of approved interventions.
- **Eligibility Rule:** The formal criteria for inclusion.
- **Budget:** Financial constraint.
- **Grant:** The donor funding mechanism.

## Synonyms
- **Project:** Often used interchangeably with Programme, though a Programme may technically contain multiple Projects.
- **Response Plan:** Synonym for a major programme strategy.

## Ambiguous Terminology
- **Needs Assessment:** Can mean macro-level population assessment (Programme) or micro-level household assessment (Case Management).
- **Targeting:** Can mean geographic selection (Programme) vs. individual eligibility (Case Management).

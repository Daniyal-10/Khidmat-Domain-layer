# Business Concepts

> **Re-adjudicated under remediation B6** against the rubric in `docs/00-governance/STAGE_5_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md` §6. Five concepts changed classification. The most consequential correction: `Programme` was classified as Operational Knowledge here while Human Owner decision **CL-001 (2026-07-27)** had ratified it as *"a distinct humanitarian business concept"* that *"shall remain independent throughout the Khidmat Foundation and future Ontology Design."* A discovery classification cannot contradict a ratified governance decision. No concept was added or removed.

## Reality Knowledge
- Geographic Area
- Target Population
- **Programme** — *reclassified from Operational (B6). Ratified as a distinct humanitarian business concept by CL-001; corroborated externally by TD-01 BD-TD01-003 (Medium-High, consistent across all Tier B sources) and described as a real ecosystem structure in `HUMANITARIAN_BUSINESS_REFERENCE_MODEL.md` Ch1. Passes rubric Q2 in any case: a programme determines what assistance exists to be received.*
- **Eligibility Rule** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). An eligibility rule is the single most direct determinant of who receives assistance. `KNOWLEDGE_TRANSFORMATION_PATTERNS.md` §4.5 traces it as the terminal constraint in the chain from donor mandate to support plan approval, and records the ethical friction it produces. Its omission changes the fairness of every case-level decision.*
- **Intervention Offering** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). Defines what assistance is available. Owned here; referenced by Case Management.*
- **Intervention Catalogue** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2), as the bounded set of Intervention Offerings a Programme makes available. Reclassified with Intervention Offering rather than separately, since the two stand or fall together.*
- **Grant** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). A grant carries funding restrictions that constrain sector, geography, target population and — where the giving form imposes one — recipient category. `programme-management/12-domain-invariants.md` states as an invariant that restricted funding cannot be shifted "regardless of urgent ground need," which is a statement about who can be helped. Now discovered in `docs/02-discovery/giving-resource-origin/` under remediation B4.*
- **Sector** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q1). TD-06 BD-TD06-001 (Tier B, High confidence) establishes Sector as one of three universally recognised dimensions along which humanitarian need is categorised, aligned to IASC clusters. It categorises what kind of human need is addressed and exists independently of any organisation's catalogue.*

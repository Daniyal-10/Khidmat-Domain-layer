# Business Lifecycles

## The Core Cognitive Lifecycle
The domain operates a non-linear, looping lifecycle for an individual case:
1. **Screening and Intake**
2. **Needs Assessment**
3. **Support Planning**
4. **Execution / Handoff** (Implementation via Resource & Logistics)
5. **Monitoring**
6. **Reassessment** (Loops back to Assessment or Planning based on changing context)
7. **Case Closure**

## Dual Lifecycles
There is a strict structural separation between a person's administrative status and their actual recovery:
- **Engagement Stage Lifecycle:** Tracks the administrative participation in the system (e.g., Registered, Active, Suspended, Closed).
- **Human Development Stage Lifecycle:** Tracks the individual's underlying progress toward resilience (e.g., Acute Crisis, Stabilized, Resilient).

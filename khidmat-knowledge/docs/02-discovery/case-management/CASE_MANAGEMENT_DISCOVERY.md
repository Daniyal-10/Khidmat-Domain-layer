# Case Management Domain Discovery (Executive Narrative)

The Case Management Domain exists to govern the individual and household lifecycle of humanitarian assistance. It solves the problem of understanding a specific person's or household's lived reality, identifying their vulnerabilities, determining their eligibility for assistance, and maintaining a continuous relationship with them over time.

Successful outcomes of this domain include a verified understanding of an individual's context, justified recommendations that match verified needs to available resources, and a continuity of care that adapts as the individual's situation changes.

The domain operates a core cognitive lifecycle: acquiring evidence directly from individuals, forming an understanding of their vulnerabilities, verifying claims, reasoning to justify a support plan, and continuously reassessing. It balances critical business tensions daily, such as the ethical pressure for rapid response versus the operational mandate for rigorous evidence verification.

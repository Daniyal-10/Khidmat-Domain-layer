# Open Questions

- How is the authority to approve a Support Plan operationally delegated during an acute crisis?
- What are the exact informal criteria practitioners use to determine if an intervention "worked" prior to formal reassessment?
- How are conflicting evidence sources reconciled in practice when community validation contradicts documentary proof?
- How is uncertainty formally represented when an assessor suspects a vulnerability but lacks hard evidence?
- How are duplicate households mathematically or operationally resolved when identified across distinct Organisations?
- How are conflicting assessments managed when two Organisations evaluate the same individual differently?
- How are informal community recommendations structurally validated within the formal approval chain?
- How are referrals tracked across organisations when systems lack direct interoperability?
- How is consent practically revoked and propagated once data has already been shared?
- How are vulnerable minors represented when no Legal Guardian or Caregiver is present?
- How are culturally sensitive cases (e.g., domestic abuse) managed without endangering the beneficiary?
- How are safeguarding concerns rapidly escalated outside standard Programme rules?

- **Eligibility** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). Eligibility determines who receives assistance; its omission changes the fairness and appropriateness of a humanitarian decision. Note the split: eligibility as the* determination *about a person is Reality and belongs here; the* Eligibility Rule *that governs it is owned by Programme Management, where it is also Reality Knowledge.*
- **Referral** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). A referral transfers responsibility for a person's unmet need to another provider; its omission changes whether that need is addressed at all. Cross-Organisational Coordination independently treats External Referral as a core produced concept.*
- **Intervention Offering** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q2). An intervention offering defines what assistance is available to a person. Owned by Programme Management; referenced here, and Reality Knowledge in both.*

## Operational Knowledge
- Priority — *retained as Operational pending evidence. `03b-need-model.md` §4 records that no repository source states how priority or severity is determined. Under rubric §6.3, a ranking with no stated derivation is not classified until its basis exists; it is held here rather than promoted on assumption.*
- Follow-up
- Case Note
- Decision Justification
- **Escalation** — *retained as Operational. The mechanics of escalation (queues, routing, timers) are explicitly Operational per `PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md` Ch7.2. The* fact *that a conclusion's consequence class requires human review is Reality Knowledge and belongs to the Cognition treatment, not to this concept.*
- **Consent Record** — *added as the Operational counterpart to Consent, per rubric §6.3's split rule. This is not a new business concept; it is the naming of the administrative artifact that was previously conflated with the person's act.*

## Delivery Event — cross-domain classification

`Delivery Event` was previously listed here as Reality Knowledge while `resource-logistics/03-concepts.md` listed `Delivery Confirmation / Receipt` as Operational Knowledge for the same phenomenon. Resolved under B6: **Delivery Event is Reality Knowledge** (an occurrence in a person's life — rubric Q1) and is **owned by Resource & Logistics**, consistent with `GLOSSARY.md`'s Support Delivery Terms. It is removed from this domain's list and referenced from its owner, per Rule AR-3. The receipt artifact remains Operational.

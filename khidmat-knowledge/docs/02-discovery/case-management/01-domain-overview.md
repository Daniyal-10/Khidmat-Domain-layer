# Domain Overview: Case Management

## Purpose
The Case Management Domain exists to govern the individual and household lifecycle of humanitarian assistance. It solves the problem of understanding a specific person's or household's lived reality, identifying their vulnerabilities, determining their eligibility for assistance, and maintaining a continuous relationship with them over time.

## Mission
To ensure an individual receives appropriate, timely support based on verified truth rather than assumption, and that their administrative engagement accurately reflects their real-world recovery progress.

## Scope
Focuses on the micro-level assessment, planning, and monitoring of individual cases. It interacts directly with affected populations to gather claims, consent, and context.

## Responsibilities
- Screening and Intake
- Identity Resolution & Duplicate Investigation
- Consent Management
- Needs Assessment & Vulnerability Reassessment
- Evidence Gathering & Documentation Validation
- Household Composition Review
- Safeguarding Review
- Support Planning
- Referral Coordination
- Monitoring & Follow-up Planning
- Case Note Maintenance
- Case Closure & Exception Handling

## Business Outcomes
- Verified understanding of an individual's context.
- Justified recommendations matching verified needs to available resources.
- Continuity of care adapting to the individual's changing situation.

## Business Capabilities
- Evidence & Knowledge Acquisition
- Understanding Formation
- Reasoning & Justified Recommendation
- Verification
- Continuity & Re-assessment

## Core Business Activities
- Registering individuals.
- Capturing claims and consent.
- Synthesizing field observations and evidence.
- Developing support plans.
- Monitoring individual recovery and vulnerability over time.

# Domain Boundaries: Case Management

## Owns
- Individual intake and assessment.
- Support planning (matching needs to interventions).
- Verification of individual claims.
- Ongoing monitoring of the individual.

## Consumes
- **From Programme Management:** Eligibility rules, intervention catalogues, and operational budgets.
- **From Resource & Logistics:** Fulfillment status indicating aid was delivered.

## Produces
- **For Resource & Logistics:** Triggered execution plans for fulfillment.

## Dependencies
- Dependent on Programme Management for rules and budgets.
- Dependent on Resource & Logistics to physically deliver the aid.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Regional needs assessments and budgeting (Programme Management).
- Procurement and financial disbursement (Resource & Logistics).
- Long-term systemic impact evaluation and independent grievance handling (Accountability & Evaluation).
- Deduplication across NGOs (Cross-Organisational Coordination).

# Exceptions
- **Emergency Necessity / Consent Deferral:** Action may proceed without explicit, documented consent if doing so is strictly required to preserve life or immediate safety. This exception is governed by necessity, proportionality, collection of minimum required information, transparency to the person as soon as possible, and continued human accountability. It is never a permanent substitute for consent.
- **Verification Impossibility:** In acute crisis, formal evidence may be impossible to gather, relying entirely on community validation or assumed truth.
- **Household Splitting:** A household may dynamically fracture during a crisis, requiring cases to split and merge context on the fly.

# Information Requirements

To perform its business activities, the Case Management domain requires:
- **Identity & Context:** Who the person is, their household structure, and their environment.
- **Claims:** What the person states they need or have experienced.
- **Evidence/Observations:** Corroborating proof supporting or refuting claims.
- **Operational Rules & Budgets:** The constraints and catalogues passed down from Programme Management.
- **Fulfillment Status:** Confirmation from Resource & Logistics that aid was delivered.

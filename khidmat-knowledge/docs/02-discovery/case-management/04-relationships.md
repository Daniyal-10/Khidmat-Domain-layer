# Business Relationships

- An **Individual** participates in a **Household**.
- A **Household** contains multiple **Individuals**.
- A **Caregiver** represents a **Dependent Individual**.
- A **Case Worker** manages a **Case**.
- A **Case** tracks a person's **Engagement Stage** independent of their **Human Development Stage**.
- A **Case** produces **Decisions**.
- A **Need** belongs to a **Case**.
- A **Claim** requires **Evidence**.
- **Evidence** supports a **Decision**.
- An **Observation** contributes to an **Assessment**.
- A **Vulnerability** generates a **Need**.
- A **Risk** influences a **Priority**.
- **Consent** authorizes an **Activity**.
- A **Decision** produces a **Decision Justification**.
- A **Need** justifies a **Support Plan**.
- A **Support Plan** includes an **Intervention**.
- An **Intervention** addresses a **Need**.
- A **Referral** transfers responsibility to a **Referral Partner**.

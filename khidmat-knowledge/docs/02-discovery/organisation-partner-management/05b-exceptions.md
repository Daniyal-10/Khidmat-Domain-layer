# 8. Exceptions

- **Informal Grassroots Partnership:** Collaborating with an unregistered community network during an acute crisis because they are the only ones with physical access, bypassing standard legal due diligence. This exception is strictly governed by necessity, proportionality, collection of the minimum information required, transparency to the person as soon as possible, and continued human accountability.
- **Government Mandated Partnerships:** A sovereign host country forces an INGO to partner with a specific, state-approved local NGO, regardless of the INGO's independent vetting.
- **Emergency Suspension of Vetting:** Bypassing standard 3-month vetting cycles to authorize an emergency medical NGO within 24 hours during an earthquake. This exception is strictly governed by necessity, proportionality, collection of the minimum information required, transparency to the person as soon as possible, and continued human accountability.

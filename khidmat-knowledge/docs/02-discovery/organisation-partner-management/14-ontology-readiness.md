# 18. Ontology Readiness

The following conceptual clusters appear highly stable and ready for subsequent formal Ontology Design (Stage 6):
- **The Institutional Entity:** Organisation and its attributes (Legal Registration, Capabilities, Geographic Presence).
- **The Relational Edge:** Partnership and Consortium acting as formal, time-bound bonds between Organisations.
- **The Trust State:** The Vetting Status of an organisation that gates downstream actions across the entire ecosystem.

These concepts can be modelled without requiring implementation assumptions.

# 1. Purpose, Outcomes, Capabilities, and Activities

## Purpose
The Organisation and Partner Management Domain exists to establish and govern the humanitarian organisational ecosystem. While other domains focus on the beneficiaries requiring aid (Registration, Case) or the aid itself (Programme, Logistics), this domain exclusively governs the *entities* providing the aid. It answers the foundational questions: *Who is operating in this crisis? What is their legal mandate? What are their capabilities? Who trusts whom?*

## Business Outcomes
- A universally trusted, foundational registry of institutional actors operating within a humanitarian context.
- Formalized, legally compliant partnership networks (MoUs, consortia) that enable safe collaboration.
- Governed suspension or revocation of trust when an organisation violates humanitarian principles.

## Business Capabilities
- Organisational Identity & Profile Management
- Partnership & Consortium Governance
- Capability & Geographic Presence Tracking
- Accreditation, Vetting & Trust Management
- Institutional Mandate & Sector Alignment

## Core Business Activities
- Vetting and legally onboarding local and international NGOs.
- Drafting, signing, and managing Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) between entities.
- Continuously tracking the fluctuating geographic presence and technical capabilities of partner organisations.
- Resolving disputes between consortium partners.
- Formally suspending trust in an organisation following a severe compliance breach (e.g., aid diversion).

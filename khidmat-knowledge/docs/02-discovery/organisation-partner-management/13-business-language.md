# 17. Terminology

## Preferred Terms
- **Organisation:** The umbrella term for any institutional entity (NGO, INGO, CBO, UN).
- **Partnership:** A formalized, legal relationship between two Organisations.
- **Consortium:** A formal group of Organisations acting together under a single grant.
- **Due Diligence:** The act of verifying an organisation's legal and financial integrity.

## Acronyms & Frameworks
- **Implementing Partner (IP) / Sub-grantee:** A local NGO executing work on behalf of an INGO.
- **Vetting:** Synonym for Due Diligence.

## Ambiguous Terminology
- **Capacity:** Can mean technical skill (Capability) or financial size. Here, it is disambiguated into "Capability" (skills) and "Scale" (size).
- **Local Partner:** Often used dismissively to mean a subcontractor, though they are sovereign organisations.

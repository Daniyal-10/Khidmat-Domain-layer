# 6. Knowledge Patterns

- **The Institutional Trust Triad:** Legal Registration (State) + Due Diligence (Financial) + Operational Vetting (Humanitarian) = Institutional Trust. If any pillar collapses, the partnership fails.
- **The Localization Paradox:** Donors demand high Compliance which favors INGOs, but demand high Community Access which favors LNGOs, forcing mandatory Partnerships between vastly unequal entities.

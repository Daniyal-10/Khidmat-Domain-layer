# 2. Stakeholders

## Actors (Enduring Participants)
- **International Non-Governmental Organisation (INGO):** A massive, well-funded foreign entity.
- **Local / National NGO (LNGO):** A domestic entity with deep community roots but often limited funding.
- **Donor / Funder:** An institutional entity providing the financial capital.
- **Government Authority:** The sovereign state actor providing the legal right to operate.
- **Community-Based Organisation (CBO):** An informal, grassroots collective often lacking formal legal registration.

## Roles (Transient Responsibilities)
- **Partnership Manager:** The role responsible for brokering, drafting, and maintaining MoUs.
- **Compliance / Vetting Officer:** The role responsible for anti-terrorism and financial due diligence checks.
- **Country Director / Head of Mission:** The executive role holding the ultimate legal and reputational liability for partnerships.

# 10. Business Events

- **Organisation Registered:** An entity is formally recognized in the ecosystem.
- **Due Diligence Passed / Failed:** An entity completes vetting.
- **Partnership Formalized:** An MoU is signed.
- **Consortium Formed:** Multiple entities legally bind together for a specific objective.
- **Capability Updated:** An NGO announces they now have surgical capacity or have opened a new field office.
- **Trust Suspended:** A partnership is halted due to allegations of fraud, safeguarding abuse, or neutrality violations.

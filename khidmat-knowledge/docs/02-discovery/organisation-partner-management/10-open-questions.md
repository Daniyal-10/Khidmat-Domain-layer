# 14. Open Questions

- How is a "Consortium" structurally represented when it acts as a single legal entity to a Donor, but operates as 5 independent NGOs on the ground?
- When a Host Government revokes an INGO's registration, how does this domain instantly propagate that legal "death" to all downstream operational domains?
- How is trust established and documented with informal, community-led grassroots organizations that structurally cannot pass Western financial audits?

# 16. Domain Invariants

- **The Liability Anchor:** Legal and financial liability always flows upward to the primary grant holder, regardless of how many sub-partners execute the work.
- **Trust is Asymmetric:** A massive INGO demands rigorous audits from a tiny LNGO, but the LNGO has no power to audit the INGO.
- **Identity Precedes Action:** An Organisation must formally exist and be recognized before it can legally hold a Programme mandate or distribute Resources.

# 11. Business Lifecycles

## The Partnership Lifecycle
1. **Identification:** Discovering an organisation with aligned mandates and complementary capabilities.
2. **Vetting / Due Diligence:** Auditing finances, legal status, and safeguarding policies.
3. **Formalization:** Drafting and signing the MoU.
4. **Active Collaboration:** The period of joint operational execution.
5. **Review / Renewal:** Evaluating the partnership's success.
6. **Dissolution:** Formally ending the legal relationship.

## The Organisational Trust Lifecycle
1. **Unknown:** No verified information exists.
2. **Vetted:** Cleared for low-risk interaction.
3. **Trusted Partner:** Cleared for high-risk interaction (sensitive data sharing, fund transfers).
4. **Suspended:** Trust temporarily revoked pending investigation.
5. **Blacklisted:** Permanent ban due to severe violations (e.g., terrorism financing).

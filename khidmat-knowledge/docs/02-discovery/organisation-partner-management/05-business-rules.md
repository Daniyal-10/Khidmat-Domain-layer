# 7. Policies and Constraints

## Policies
- **Universal:** Organisations must adhere strictly to the core humanitarian principles: Humanity, Neutrality, Impartiality, and Independence.
- **Donor:** "Localization" targets mandate that a specific percentage of funding must flow through Local NGOs rather than International NGOs.
- **Organisation:** No funds or sensitive data can be transferred to an entity that has not passed Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Anti-Terrorism Financing (ATF) checks.

## Constraints
- **Legal:** Operating without formal Government accreditation can lead to the arrest of staff and seizure of assets.
- **Political:** Maintaining "Neutrality" is exceptionally difficult when a local NGO is deeply embedded in a marginalized community actively fighting the state.
- **Financial:** Small grassroots organisations often lack the sophisticated accounting systems required to pass INGO/Donor due diligence audits.

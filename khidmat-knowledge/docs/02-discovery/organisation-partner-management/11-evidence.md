# 15. Discovery Evidence

## Established Facts
- The humanitarian system is heavily fractured; partnerships are a survival necessity, not an optional luxury.
- INGOs bear the ultimate legal and financial liability for the actions of their local LNGO partners.
- Trust is not transitive. If NGO A trusts NGO B, and NGO B trusts NGO C, it does not mean NGO A legally trusts NGO C.

## Reasonable Assumptions
- State actors in conflict zones will frequently use legal registration as a weapon to block aid to specific populations.
- Organisations will frequently overstate their capabilities to secure partnership funding.

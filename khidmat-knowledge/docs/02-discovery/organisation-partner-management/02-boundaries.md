# 3. Domain Dependencies and Boundaries

## Owns
- The definition and existence of an Organisation.
- The formal legal and operational relationships between Organisations (Partnerships, Consortia).
- The vetted trust level and accreditation status of an Organisation.
- The mapping of Organisational capabilities and geographic presence.

## Consumes
- **From External Regulators:** Legal registration certificates, anti-terrorism blacklists.
- **From Accountability & Evaluation:** Severe grievance reports that might trigger a partnership suspension.

## Produces
- **For Cross-Organisational Coordination:** The vetted "Trust Topology" (knowing exactly which NGOs are cleared to receive sensitive referrals).
- **For Programme Management:** The capability constraints of implementing partners (used to determine if a programme is feasible).
- **For Resource & Logistics:** Vetted entities cleared to act as distribution partners.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Brokering individual beneficiary referrals (Cross-Organisational Coordination).
- Designing the interventions the partners will execute (Programme Management).
- Monitoring the long-term impact of the partner's work (Accountability & Evaluation).

# 9. Business Tensions

The Organisation & Partner Management domain continuously balances competing institutional forces:
- **Compliance vs. Localization:** The intense donor pressure to fund local NGOs versus the reality that local NGOs often fail the rigid Western compliance and due diligence standards.
- **Trust vs. Liability:** The ethical desire to trust a partner NGO versus the legal reality that the originating INGO bears the financial liability if the partner commits fraud.
- **Collaboration vs. Competition:** Organisations form Consortia to secure massive grants together, while simultaneously competing against each other for institutional dominance and future funding.
- **Neutrality vs. Advocacy:** The friction between remaining politically neutral (to maintain government access) and partnering with local civil society groups that actively advocate against the government.

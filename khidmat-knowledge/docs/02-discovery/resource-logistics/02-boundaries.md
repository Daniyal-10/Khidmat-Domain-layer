# Domain Boundaries: Resource and Logistics

## Owns
- Supply chain management.
- Warehousing and inventory.
- Procurement and vendor management.
- Cash transfer execution.
- Physical distribution operations.

## Consumes
- **From Case Management:** Execution triggers (approved Support Plans) indicating who needs what and where.

## Produces
- **For Case Management:** Fulfillment status (confirmation that aid was delivered or failed).

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Assessing beneficiary vulnerability (Case Management).
- Defining program eligibility rules and budgets (Programme Management).
- Monitoring long-term beneficiary outcomes (Accountability & Evaluation).
- Handling beneficiary grievances regarding aid quality (Accountability & Evaluation).

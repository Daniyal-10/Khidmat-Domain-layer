# Business Relationships

- A **Warehouse** stores **Inventory**.
- A **Procurement Request** generates a **Purchase Order**.
- A **Purchase Order** is fulfilled by a **Vendor**.
- An **Execution Trigger** demands an **Intervention Offering**.
- A **Shipment** transfers **Inventory** to a **Distribution Point**.
- A **Distribution Point** facilitates **Delivery Confirmation**.
- A **Financial Service Provider** executes a **Cash Transfer**.
- **Inventory** fulfills an **Execution Trigger**.

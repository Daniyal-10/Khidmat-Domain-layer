# Business Events

- **Procurement Initiated:** A need for stock is formally requested.
- **Goods Received:** Items arrive at a warehouse and enter inventory.
- **Stock Alert Triggered:** Inventory falls below minimum thresholds or nears expiration.
- **Execution Trigger Received:** Case Management requests the delivery of an approved Support Plan.
- **Dispatch Authorized:** Goods are assigned to a waybill and leave the warehouse.
- **Goods in Transit:** Physical movement of material.
- **Cash Transfer Instructed:** FSP is ordered to release funds to a beneficiary.
- **Fulfillment Confirmed:** Cryptographic or physical signature verifying the beneficiary received the aid.
- **Distribution Failed:** Aid could not be delivered (beneficiary absent, logistics breakdown, security incident).

# Business Language

## Preferred Terms
- **Fulfillment:** The complete act of delivering the approved intervention.
- **Execution Trigger:** The authoritative instruction from Case Management to deliver aid.
- **Waybill:** The authoritative document tracking goods in transit.
- **FSP:** Financial Service Provider (e.g., a mobile money operator or bank).

## Synonyms
- **Delivery:** Synonym for Fulfillment.
- **Beneficiary List / Distribution List:** Older synonyms for a batch of Execution Triggers.

## Ambiguous Terminology
- **Distribution:** Can ambiguously refer to the allocation of budgets (Programme Management) or the physical handing out of goods (Logistics). In this domain, it strictly means physical handover.

# Business Lifecycles

The domain manages two distinct lifecycles:

## 1. The Procurement Lifecycle (Upstream)
1. **Needs Identification:** Recognizing a deficit against Programme demand.
2. **Sourcing & Bidding:** Identifying vendors.
3. **Purchase Order:** Legally committing to buy.
4. **Receipt & Quality Control:** Accepting goods into the warehouse.
5. **Inventory:** Goods sit in active stock.

## 2. The Fulfillment Lifecycle (Downstream)
1. **Execution Trigger Received:** Case Management hands off an approved plan.
2. **Allocation:** Reserving specific stock or cash for the trigger.
3. **Dispatch:** Moving the resource to the Distribution Point or FSP.
4. **Distribution:** The physical or digital handover.
5. **Delivery Confirmation:** Formal closure of the execution loop.

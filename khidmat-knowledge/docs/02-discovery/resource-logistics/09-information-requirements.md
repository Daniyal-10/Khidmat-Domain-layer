# Information Requirements

To perform its business activities, the Resource and Logistics domain requires:
- **Execution Triggers:** Who the person is, what they are approved for, and their location (consumed from Case Management).
- **Inventory Visibility:** Real-time, accurate counts of stock across all warehouses and transit nodes.
- **Vendor/Market Data:** Catalogues, pricing, lead times, and FSP network coverage.
- **Security & Access Context:** Understanding whether a transport route or distribution point is physically safe to operate.

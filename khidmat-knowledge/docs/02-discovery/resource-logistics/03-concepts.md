# Business Concepts

> **Re-adjudicated under remediation B6** against the rubric in `docs/00-governance/STAGE_5_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md` §6. One concept was promoted and given a canonical home here, resolving a cross-domain contradiction with `case-management/03-concepts.md`. No concept was removed.

## Reality Knowledge
- Warehouse
- Distribution Point
- Financial Service Provider (FSP)
- Vendor / Supplier
- Market Capacity
- In-kind Good
- **Delivery Event** — *promoted and given its canonical home here under B6. `case-management/03-concepts.md` previously listed Delivery Event as Reality Knowledge while this domain listed Delivery Confirmation / Receipt as Operational for the same phenomenon. Resolved: the delivery is an occurrence in a person's life and survives rubric Q1; `GLOSSARY.md` Support Delivery Terms already defines Delivery Event as "the fundamental unit of execution" and assigns it to this subject matter. Owned here, referenced by Case Management, per Rule AR-3.*
- **Cash Transfer** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q1). TD-06 BD-TD06-001 (Tier B, High confidence) establishes Cash and Voucher Assistance as one of three universally recognised delivery modalities in humanitarian practice. A modality is a real form assistance takes, not an artifact of any organisation's process.*
- **Service Voucher** — *reclassified from Operational (B6) with Cash Transfer, on the same TD-06 evidence.*

## Operational Knowledge
- Stock / Inventory
- Shipment / Consignment
- Procurement Request
- Purchase Order
- Waybill / Dispatch Note
- Execution Trigger (Fulfillment Order) — *retained as Operational. `04b-knowledge-patterns.md` describes it as the point at which an authorised Support Plan "crosses the domain boundary," which is a handoff artifact between two organisational functions.*
- Delivery Confirmation / Receipt — *retained as Operational: the record of the Delivery Event, not the event itself. The* proof *carried by that record is Evidence and is Reality Knowledge under the rubric's third question.*

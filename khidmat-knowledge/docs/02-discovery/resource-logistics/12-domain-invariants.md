# Domain Invariants

Enduring truths that define Resource and Logistics:
- **Separation of Assessment and Delivery:** The entity that decides someone is eligible for aid (Case Management) cannot be the entity that physically hands out the aid (Logistics).
- **The Audit Trail:** Every single unit of material or cash must be mathematically traceable from procurement origin to beneficiary receipt.
- **Execution Without Question:** Logistics does not second-guess the vulnerability assessment; if an Execution Trigger is validly signed by Case Management, Logistics fulfills it.

# Business Rules

## Universal Rules
- **Anti-Fraud Separation of Duties:** The staff member who procures goods cannot be the same staff member who receives them into stock; the staff member who authorizes a distribution cannot physically hand out the goods.
- Aid must be delivered in a manner that ensures the physical safety and dignity of the recipient.

## Donor Rules
- Procurement often requires minimum competitive bidding (e.g., at least 3 quotes).
- Origin constraints (e.g., funds cannot be used to purchase goods from specific embargoed countries).

## Organisation Rules
- Financial thresholds dictating whether procurement can be localized or must be tendered internationally.

## Operational Constraints
- **Cold Chain:** Medical supplies require unbroken temperature-controlled logistics.
- **Liquidity:** Cash distributions in remote areas are constrained by the physical cash liquidity of local agents.

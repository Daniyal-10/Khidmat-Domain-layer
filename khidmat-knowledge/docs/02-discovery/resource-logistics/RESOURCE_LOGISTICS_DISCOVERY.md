# Resource and Logistics Domain Discovery (Executive Narrative)

The Resource and Logistics Domain exists to execute approved humanitarian support plans by managing the physical and financial flow of assistance. It bridges the gap between a justified need (assessed by Case Management) and the actual delivery of material, services, or cash to the affected person.

While Programme Management determines *what* to do and Case Management determines *who* needs it, Resource and Logistics determines *how* to source, transport, and distribute it. It handles procurement, supply chain, warehousing, cash transfers, vendor relationships, and final distribution.

Successful outcomes of this domain include the safe, timely, and dignified delivery of aid, precise auditability of all resources from origin to beneficiary, and efficient management of operational supply chains. The domain balances critical tensions, such as the speed of emergency procurement versus the compliance requirements of anti-fraud separation of duties.

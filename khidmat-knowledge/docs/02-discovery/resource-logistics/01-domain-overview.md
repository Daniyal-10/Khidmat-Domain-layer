# Domain Overview: Resource and Logistics

## Purpose
To physically and financially execute humanitarian interventions.

## Mission
Ensure that the right assistance reaches the right person at the right time, safely and efficiently, while maintaining an auditable, unbroken trail of resources from origin to beneficiary.

## Scope
The domain encompasses procurement, warehousing, inventory management, transport, cash disbursement, vendor management, and last-mile distribution.

## Responsibilities
- Sourcing goods and services (Procurement)
- Managing Vendors and Financial Service Providers (FSPs)
- Warehousing and tracking inventory
- Dispatching and transporting goods
- Managing physical Distribution Points
- Executing Cash-Based Interventions (CBIs)
- Confirming fulfillment (delivery receipt)

## Business Outcomes
- Safe, timely, and dignified delivery of approved aid.
- Precise auditability of all resources from procurement origin to beneficiary receipt.
- Efficient management of operational supply chains and financial service networks.

## Business Capabilities
- Procurement & Sourcing
- Supply Chain & Warehousing Management
- Cash Transfer & Financial Service Execution
- Physical Distribution & Last-Mile Logistics
- Fulfillment Verification

## Core Business Activities
- Sourcing quotes and selecting vendors.
- Receiving, inspecting, and storing inventory.
- Allocating stock to specific distribution points.
- Authorizing FSPs to disburse cash.
- Securing physical signatures or cryptographic proof of beneficiary receipt.

# Open Questions

- How is the physical security of a distribution point formally verified and continually reassessed before and during a dispatch?
- How are partial fulfillments structurally handled (e.g., a beneficiary is approved for a full hygiene kit, but the warehouse only has soap)? Does Case Management or Logistics own the "backorder" state?
- How does the domain manage cash liquidity crises in local markets when electronic transfers (mobile money) fail due to network outages?
- How is the return of uncollected goods (reverse logistics) managed when beneficiaries fail to appear at a distribution point?

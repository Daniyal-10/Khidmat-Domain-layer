# 3. Domain Dependencies and Boundaries

## Owns
- The foundational definition of Identity (Biographical, Biometric).
- Household **membership recording and adjudication** — establishing who is recorded as belonging to a household, including the Household Composition Decision (§12.3) and the resolution of membership disputes. *(Refined under remediation B11. This domain previously claimed "household composition and membership lifecycles" in full. The household **as a social unit** — its resilience, internal dependency structure, housing and utilities, and community context — is owned by the Human Reality domain, `docs/02-discovery/human-reality/`. The seam: this domain answers "who is recorded as belonging to this household?"; Human Reality answers "what kind of social unit is this, and what is true of it?" Recorded in `CONCEPT_OWNERSHIP.md` §9.)*
- Registration status (Active, Inactive, Deceased, Duplicated).
- Foundational Consent to be processed by the humanitarian organisation.

## Consumes
- **From Community/External Entities:** Identity evidence (passports, refugee cards, community leader validations).

## Produces
- **For Case Management:** Verified Identities and Household structures; Foundational Consent.
- **For Cross-Organisational Coordination:** Cryptographic identity hashes for deduplication against other NGOs.
- **For Accountability & Evaluation:** Verified contact information for post-distribution monitoring.

## Explicitly Out of Scope
- Assessing the vulnerability or needs of the beneficiary (Case Management).
- Determining eligibility for aid (Programme Management).
- Distributing aid (Resource and Logistics).

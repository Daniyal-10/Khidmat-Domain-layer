# 4. Business Concepts

> **Re-adjudicated under remediation B6** against the rubric in `docs/00-governance/STAGE_5_DISCOVERY_STANDARD.md` §6. One concept changed classification. No concept was added or removed.

## Reality Knowledge
- Identity Profile
- Biographical Data
- Household / Family Unit — *the social unit itself is owned by the Human Reality domain (`docs/02-discovery/human-reality/`, created under remediation B2). This domain owns the recording and adjudication of its membership as part of registry integrity. Boundary recorded in `CONCEPT_OWNERSHIP.md` §8.*
- Dependent
- Identity Evidence (Documentary, Biometric, Testimonial)
- **Foundational Consent** — *reclassified from Operational (B6, rubric Q1 and Q2). Consent is the person's own act of authorisation, which belongs to them and persists independently of any organisation recording it; `registration-identity/12-domain-invariants.md` states that "the right to exist in the database is predicated on continuous, revocable consent," and `FOUNDATION_CONCEPTS.md` §3 already treats Consent as a cross-domain foundational concept. Constitution Article IX makes consent a right of the person, not an administrative artifact. This aligns the classification with `case-management/03-concepts.md`, which already held Consent as Reality Knowledge — the contradiction is resolved in favour of the person.*

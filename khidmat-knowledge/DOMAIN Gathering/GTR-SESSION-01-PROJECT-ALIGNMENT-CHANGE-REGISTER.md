* **Experienced Volunteer Review:** Verifies the survey claims against ground reality.
* **Rejection Semantics:** A totally false claim is rejected.
* **Progression Semantics:** A genuine requirement proceeds.
* **Need Correction:** A surveyed need can be corrected from ground reality.
* **Partial Need Acceptance:** Multiple reported needs can result in partial acceptance at the need level (some genuine proceed, others do not).
* **No Second Survey:** The process moves directly from survey -> volunteer review -> decision without a second survey phase.
